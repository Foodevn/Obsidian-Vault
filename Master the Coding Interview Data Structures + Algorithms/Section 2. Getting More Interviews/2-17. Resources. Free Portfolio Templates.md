Resources: Free Portfolio Templates

We've already talked about Portfolios in a previous lesson, so I wanted to remind you of the most important things:  
  

1. It should live somewhere online and be shared on your job applications.
    
2. It should contain 1~2 BIG projects that can wow employers. If you are doing a ZTM course every course contains at least 1 big wow project you can add to your portfolio.
    

  
**If you are looking for a quick and efficient way of building a portfolio page, I recommend using an AI tool like I have shown you previously or use these free templates:**

- [HTML 5UP](https://html5up.net/) (this is my favourite)
    
- [Free CSS Templates](https://www.free-css.com/template-categories/portfolio)
    
- [Cruip](https://cruip.com/free-templates/)
    
- [Bootstrap Templates](https://mdbootstrap.com/freebies/)
    

  

A great place to share your portfolio and see other people's portfolio is in our Discord community. Have a look at the **Job Hunting** category for all related conversation regarding portfolios, job interviews, etc... Finally, you can add your own profile including your portfolio to our student led Job Board right here: [https://github.com/zero-to-mastery/ZtM-Job-Board](https://github.com/zero-to-mastery/ZtM-Job-Board)  

Finally, for extra inspiration, you can see a list of some really great portfolios [here](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/portfolio/).  
  
**Remember, it's more important what's inside your portfolio than your actual portfolio. You need to have those 1~2 BIG PROJECTS we talked about.**
